Final Year Project Summary

What is a final year project?

A final year project is the culminating academic work that allows students to transform classroom knowledge into a practical and meaningful system. It tests research ability, technical implementation, analysis, and presentation.

Historically, final year projects became an important part of university education as a way to bridge theory and practice. They help students demonstrate independence, problem solving, innovation, and readiness for professional work.

Project Overview

ASL Sign Recognition System

This project is an artificial intelligence based sign language recognition system designed to classify isolated American Sign Language signs from short video clips. It extracts hand and body pose landmarks, normalizes the movement sequence, and uses a trained Light Transformer model to predict the performed sign.
